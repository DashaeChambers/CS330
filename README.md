# CS-330 Computational Graphics and Visualization — Project Reflection

## 1. How Do I Approach Designing Software?

My approach to designing software is to break a project into smaller, manageable parts and focus on understanding the requirements before beginning development. Throughout this project, I learned how important it is to plan the layout of a 3D scene, choose appropriate shapes and colors, and use transformations to position, scale, and rotate objects. I also developed a better understanding of how camera positioning, lighting, and object placement work together to create a complete scene.

The design process I followed involved reviewing the project requirements, building the scene step by step, and making adjustments as I worked through each milestone. Rather than trying to complete everything at once, I focused on individual components and tested how they looked and functioned together. This helped me identify areas that needed improvement and better understand how each part contributed to the final result.

In future projects, I can apply this approach by planning the structure of my software before writing code, identifying the individual components I need, and considering how those components will work together. Creating a clear plan and reviewing my progress throughout development can help me build more organized, efficient, and maintainable software.

## 2. How Do I Approach Developing Programs?

While developing my 3D scene, I used strategies such as breaking the code into manageable sections, reusing existing functions, and making incremental changes to the scene. Working with C++, OpenGL, object transformations, meshes, and shaders helped me understand how programming concepts can be used to create interactive visual experiences. I also learned the importance of understanding existing code before making changes, especially when working with an established project structure.

Iteration played an important role in my development process. I frequently needed to make adjustments, run the program, observe the results, and troubleshoot problems when the scene did not behave or appear as expected. Some changes required additional debugging and testing before I achieved the intended result. These experiences taught me that development is rarely a one-step process and that mistakes can provide valuable opportunities to learn.

Throughout the milestones, my approach to coding evolved from focusing primarily on completing individual requirements to thinking more carefully about how different parts of the program interact. I became more comfortable reviewing code, identifying problems, and making targeted changes instead of modifying multiple sections at once. Completing the project helped me recognize the value of patience, persistence, testing, and incremental improvement when developing software.

## 3. How Can Computer Science Help Me Reach My Goals?

Computational graphics and visualizations have expanded my understanding of how computer science can be used to create realistic objects, interactive environments, and visual representations of information. Through this project, I gained experience with 3D modeling concepts, coordinate systems, transformations, cameras, shaders, and graphics programming. These skills can support my future education by helping me understand more advanced programming concepts and giving me practical experience applying what I learn in my coursework.

Professionally, my background and interest in software engineering and technology make these skills valuable because they demonstrate my ability to solve problems, learn unfamiliar technologies, and develop applications through a structured process. Computational graphics can be applied in areas such as simulation, data visualization, user interface development, training environments, and interactive software. Although my career interests extend beyond graphics programming, the problem-solving, debugging, and programming skills I developed are transferable to many areas of technology.

Overall, this project helped me become more confident in my ability to work through technical challenges and complete a complex programming assignment. It reinforced the importance of planning, testing, and continuous learning. I can carry these lessons into future coursework and professional projects as I continue developing my skills in computer science and software engineering.
